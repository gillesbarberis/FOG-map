About the research

Each FOG episode starts with a place and follows its musical connections: local scenes, independent labels and the artists who shape them. Selections, field material and guest contributions bring those connections into a radio format.

How to read the atlas

The atlas connects recordings, tracklist credits and documented label relationships. An appearance in an episode does not establish an artist’s nationality or home base. Locations may be approximate; sources and editorial decisions are recorded in the research notes.

01

Music & Collaboration

I develop electronic music through solo work and ongoing exchanges with other artists. Listening and responding to one another changes the material and the direction of a piece.

Current collaborations include Arsène Martinet, Sorm and Recy.

02

Spatial Practice

SPAT / SERRA MADRE · BOLOGNA

Zoonoses

Originally released on AEDI Records, Zoonoses was reopened in Ableton for Serra Madre: Immersive Listening, working with SPAT. Movement and reverberation began to reshape the composition itself.

A released work became material for a new spatial reading.

DOLBY ATMOS / ATMOS 360 · FONOPRINT

Acusmatiq material

An excerpt from a partly improvised Acusmatiq live set was developed during Atmos 360 at Fonoprint. With the material still taking shape, sound selection, composition and spatial mixing developed together.

Spatial decisions entered while the music was still being composed.

04

Professional Audio

Film sound / preservation

For nearly two decades I have worked in film sound restoration and post-production at L’Immagine Ritrovata / Cineteca di Bologna. My practice includes Pro Tools, sound restoration and post-production. Working with historical sound has shaped how I think about artistic intention as formats and playback systems change.

IN DEVELOPMENT / NOT YET OPERATIONAL

A future Dolby Atmos cinema mixing room is in development for the new L’Immagine Ritrovata site, with d&b infrastructure under consideration. This prospective environment connects with my exploration of spatial composition across different systems.

05

Instrument R&D

TENS

TENS is an ongoing family of experimental electroacoustic instruments exploring multiple independent outputs. The research considers how gesture and the sound source can shape spatial possibilities from the start.

Ongoing independent R&D project. Digital processing and Max for Live are research directions.

From 2020 to 2022, I assembled and sold hundreds of DIY Eurorack modules. That hands-on hardware experience forms part of the background to TENS.

06

FOG / Radio & Research

FOG is a monthly radio project built through musical geography: research into local scenes, labels and listening cultures, combined with selections, field material and contributions from artists based in each place. The atlas traces the people, places and relationships encountered along the way.
