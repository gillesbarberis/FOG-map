FOG / CURRENT PRACTICE

Sound as material,
space as composition.

FOG extends beyond the radio atlas into an evolving practice connecting music, collaboration, immersive sound, professional film audio and experimental instruments.

01

MUSIC & COLLABORATION

Electronic music developed through solo work and ongoing exchanges with other artists. Collaboration is part of the compositional method: material changes through listening, response and mutual transformation.

Current collaborations include Arsène Martinet, Sorm and Recy.

02

SPATIAL PRACTICE

Zoonoses / SPAT

Originally released on AEDI Records, Zoonoses was later reopened in Ableton for Serra Madre: Immersive Listening in Bologna. Movement and reverberation became more than placement decisions and began to reshape parts of the composition.

Acusmatiq material / Dolby Atmos

An excerpt from a partly improvised live set was developed during Atmos 360 at Fonoprint. The material was still embryonic: sound selection, composition and spatial decisions developed together inside the immersive process.

03

CURRENT QUESTION

If a work is conceived for immersive listening, how can space become an integral part of composition from its beginning — without allowing a single spatial system to define the compositional language?

The next step is to explore spatial decisions as part of shared listening, response and composition, rather than as a final layer applied to finished material.

04

PROFESSIONAL PRACTICE

Nearly two decades in film sound restoration and post-production at L’Immagine Ritrovata / Cineteca di Bologna have shaped an approach to sound in which artistic intention, technical formats and playback systems are never quite the same thing.

Current professional context is also moving toward immersive cinema workflows, alongside the development of a new Dolby Atmos cinema mixing environment with d&b-based infrastructure under consideration.

05

INSTRUMENT R&D

TENS is an ongoing family of experimental electroacoustic instruments. Current prototypes explore multiple independent outputs and the possibility of treating separation, routing and spatial behaviour as properties of the source and gesture — before a renderer is chosen.

Public documentation intentionally remains high-level while the instruments are in development.

06

FOG / RADIO & RESEARCH

FOG is a monthly radio project built through musical geography: research into local scenes, labels and listening cultures, combined with selections, field material and contributions from artists based in each place.
