About the research

Each FOG episode starts with a place and follows its musical connections: local scenes, independent labels and the artists who shape them. Selections, field material and guest contributions bring those connections into a radio format.

How to read the atlas

The atlas connects recordings, tracklist credits and documented label relationships. An appearance in an episode does not establish an artist’s nationality or home base. Locations may be approximate; sources and editorial decisions are recorded in the research notes.

GILLES BARBERIS / FOG / CURRENT PRACTICE

Music, research
& sound practice.

I’m Gilles Barberis. My work connects electronic music culture, experimental instruments and professional audio — from radio research to film sound and spatial listening. My current research asks how a sound work can be passed on and re-performed as its technical environment changes.

Music & Collaboration

I develop electronic music through solo work and ongoing exchanges with other artists. Listening and responding to one another changes the material and the direction of a piece.

Zuma Studio is my duo with Enrico “Lizard” Sandron. Our release Things That Almost Exist (Secuencias Temporales, 2026) includes four original tracks and two interpretations by Arsène Martinet.

Current artistic exchanges include Arsène Martinet, Sorm and Recy. These relationships do not imply participation in the research proposal below.

DOLBY ATMOS / ATMOS 360 · FONOPRINT

Acusmatiq material

My practical work during Atmos 360 at Fonoprint used an excerpt from the live material developed for Acusmatiq. Composition, sound selection and spatial mixing could be explored together within a Dolby Atmos production exercise.

Completed practical training in 2026. The Acusmatiq live performance and the Atmos exercise are distinct activities; Avid Dolby Atmos certification has not yet been obtained.

03
RESEARCH PROPOSAL / NOT YET REALISED

Preserving Spatial Intent

Rehearsing the Afterlife of a Sound Work

A proposed new spatial composition that rehearses its own future re-performance. With a collaborating artist, I would document its essential relationships and test an alternative realisation from a spatial score and source assets, withholding the original session for one reconstruction exercise.

The question is not whether every renderer can reproduce the same coordinates, but what another practitioner needs to carry forward the relationships that matter in this particular work. Listening positions, permitted movement and the collective listening arrangement belong in that account alongside technical dependencies.

Futures of Listening, SpatDIF, ZiMMT/SANE, IRCAM Sidney and media-conservation research provide relevant context. They are references, not partners or affiliations. No residency or research collaborator is confirmed.

05

Professional Audio

Film sound / preservation

For nearly two decades I have worked in film sound restoration and post-production at L’Immagine Ritrovata / Cineteca di Bologna. My practice includes Pro Tools, sound restoration and post-production. Working with historical sound has shaped how I think about artistic intention as formats and playback systems change.

IN DEVELOPMENT / NOT YET OPERATIONAL

A future Dolby Atmos cinema mixing room is in development for the new L’Immagine Ritrovata site, with d&b infrastructure under consideration. This prospective environment connects with my exploration of spatial composition across different systems; it is not an available facility for the current proposal.

06

Instrument R&D

TENS

TENS is an ongoing family of experimental electroacoustic instruments exploring strings, pickups and multiple independent outputs. The research considers how gesture and the sound source can shape spatial possibilities from the start.

Ongoing independent R&D. Digital processing, Max for Live and spatial control are exploratory directions, not a finished or validated controller. TENS may contribute material to Preserving Spatial Intent, but the proposed work does not depend on its completion.

From 2020 to 2022, I assembled and sold hundreds of DIY Eurorack modules. That hands-on hardware experience forms part of the background to TENS.

FOG / Radio & Research

Co-created with Effe, FOG is a monthly radio project built through musical geography: research into local scenes, labels and listening cultures, combined with selections, field material and artist contributions. The atlas traces the people, places and relationships encountered along the way.
